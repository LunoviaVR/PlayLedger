Playtime Tracker 3.0.1 makes the dashboard easier to use and nicer to look at: every click works the first time, text is smooth, and the glass look is truly see-through.

**Download:** `Setup.exe` below. If you have 3.0.0 or 2.2.0, it updates itself when no game is running.

## Fixed
- **One click is enough.** Buttons, games, sessions, days, switches and menu items sometimes needed two clicks before they responded. Now the first click always works.
- **Smoother text.** Text was drawn snapped to the pixel grid, which looked too sharp and thin. It's now drawn the way the rest of Windows draws it.
- **Glass effects are see-through.** With **Glass effects** on, the window only got darker. Now Windows' Acrylic material shows what's behind the window, softly blurred, with translucent cards over it. It needs **Hardware acceleration** on and Windows 11 version 22H2 or later.
- **Even navigation bar.** When folded to icons, the navigation bar is narrower and its icons sit in the middle.
- **Playing now** shows just the time played, without "so far".
- **No leftover highlight.** The outline around a clicked item stayed after selecting it. Outlines now only show while you move around with the keyboard (Tab), as in the rest of Windows.
- **SteamGridDB pictures scroll.** When choosing artwork from SteamGridDB, the grid was cut off and couldn't be scrolled. Now it starts at the top and scrolls to every picture.

## Changed
- The support button is now simply **Tip**, with the Cash App logo.
